Incident write-ups

Three failures from that cluster, written up the way I would write them up for a team. They are the honest version: what I thought was broken, and what actually was.

  • 422 restarts from a single character

    A = where the kubelet wanted a < put k3s in a restart loop for six hours — while the workloads kept serving traffic, which is exactly what made it hard to see.

  • The API that its own liveness probe killed

    Thousands of "no free DB connection" lines in the log, and every one of them was a symptom rather than the cause. The trigger was a burst from the frontend and a synchronous handler holding a ten-slot pool.

  • An SSO login page served as a container image

    Cloudflare Access answering /v2/ with a redirect broke Flux and Trivy at once. The fix turned on a detail of how the registry derives its auth realm.
